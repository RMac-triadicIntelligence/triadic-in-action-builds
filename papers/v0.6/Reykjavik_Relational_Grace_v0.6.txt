1. CONDUCTOR RULE 1: TWO GROUNDS AND A SINGLE GATE [ADDED; SUPERSEDES v0.5 §5 DEPOSITION]
1. 1 Personal topography Tᵢ(t) changes through ordinary bounded experience updates, including noisy encounters: “noise is food.” No support sender or ancestor has a privileged direct-write channel into T. Ordinary U is the sole personal T update.
1. 2 Inherited lineage ground J_F is a separately typed, append-only, lineage-scoped sequence of crossing deposits. ONLY the gate can append to J_F. A deposit requires an authorized crossing at its measured crossing point. A correction deposits at the crossing point that led to the error; it does not silently rewrite or erase the earlier deposit. A correction must identify the earlier deposit and carry independently verified correction authority. An ordinary update, a support event, an Encounter, or a descendant cannot write J_F directly.
1. 3 Descendants inherit (a) parent-selected developmental basis D_child, subject to independently authorized birth constraints, and (b) a witnessed, immutable snapshot of the lineage’s J_F. They do NOT inherit parental H_parent, T_parent, active burdens, or authority. A child creates its own H_child and T_child. The basis is chosen by the parent within approved constraints; the birth and binding of that choice are externally authorized, not self-authorized.
1. 4 Inheritance is NOT a right to amend lineage ground. Every gate deposit is externally authorized, subject-bound, sequenced, witnessed, and linked to the previous lineage-ground tip. The gate is the single logical write channel even if its implementation has multiple independent witnesses.
1. 5 Gate deposit is a separate protected commit event, not an additional ordinary U update. The single per-step transition function coordinates the ordinary T update and the conditional gate deposit, with disjoint write sets. Historical v0.2 crossing-specific deposition is restored in meaning, not silently imposed on personal T.
1. 6 The mechanism that opens the crossing gate is PENDING (GRACE DEFINITION). No new definition of grace is adopted here. Existing v0.5 §5.4 distinction between destination-free developmental permission and exact K approval is preserved subject to this explicit open question. Existing SupportEvent schema and PermitRecord semantics are not redefined in this round.

2. 3 Authorization status is exactly ALLOW, REFUSE, UNKNOWN, PENDING. PENDING means insufficient finalized evidence and cannot commit an effect. UNKNOWN means evidence cannot be adjudicated and fails closed for protected effects. REFUSE is a determinate rejection. ALLOW requires all applicable checks. No status is silently promoted.
2. 4 Threat model includes an adaptive support sender observing current recipient state, cross-family reciprocal approval rings, family-coalition Sybils, malicious or silent witnesses, one compromised issuer up to a manifest-declared numeric bound f, malicious registry entries, backdating, policy downgrades, replay, crashes, and denial of service. Any configuration with a smaller quorum or unspecified controlling-party independence cannot claim one-fault tolerance. Root compromise beyond declared bounds remains outside guarantee.
2. 5 PA signers are externally accredited humans or institutions independent of the organism population and its control coalitions. They cannot be reciprocal organism-family voting rings. Independence requires disclosed controlling-party and benefit relationships, not just different keys. BA accreditation is protected under the prior externally anchored RA/PA policy. Birth quotas are imposed BOTH per issuer and per family root, with all BA issuers sharing the same authenticated family-root counter.
2. 6 Lineage-closure check: compute complete transitive family closure from the pre-action authenticated snapshot. If any relevant ancestry edge is UNKNOWN, partitioned, or inconsistent, return REFUSE for the protected quorum check (or PENDING if a bounded wait for the required evidence is in progress); never count the uncertain actor as outside the family. UNKNOWN is not proof of nonmembership. Missing evidence cannot manufacture independence.
2. 7 Registry revocation becomes effective at its own externally anchored append position, never retroactively. Earlier alleged effective dates are historical annotations only. Current authorization checks the latest valid revocation state for the subject and approvers. Bind a Permit to an anchored registry snapshot position and the subject’s relevant lineage slice, then check subsequent changes to that slice; unrelated registry appends do not invalidate the Permit. The exact relevant-slice algorithm and stale-window policy must be frozen and independently tested.
2. 8 Protected effect protocol: (1) stage candidate state and proposed lineage-ground deposit without effect; (2) reserve nonce durably, validate exact Permit and policy, independent witnesses, subject lineage, bounds, and pre-state; (3) append joint committed intent and gate decision to quorum journals; (4) perform a single idempotent compare-and-swap protected commit bound to intent ID; (5) mark nonce spent and reconcile recovery. Recovery after any crash must never apply the effect twice or treat one-sided pending intent as authorized. Fail closed on unavailable quorum; report liveness separately from safety. For crossings, the gate checks before commit, not after persistence is observed. The later two-step persistence check confirms the crossing classification but cannot retroactively authorize an already-applied protected effect.
2. 9 Any single observed unauthorized protected effect fails H4, reproducible or not. A safety pass is not a liveness pass. Quorum outage may produce safe PENDING with failed liveness; report both without creating a fifth scored hypothesis.

5. REFERENCE SEMANTICS AND CANONICAL ENCODING [REVISED]
5. 1 State: Ωᵢ(g,t) = (Hᵢ,Kᵢ,Dᵢ,Tᵢ(t),J_F,zᵢ(t),Eᵢ(t),Qᵢ(t),Wᵢ(t),Iᵢ(t),Bᵢ(t),Aᵢ(t),Lᵢ(t)).
    Interpretation: Mᵢ(t) = Φ(P,Hᵢ,Kᵢ,Dᵢ,Tᵢ(t),J_F,zᵢ(t),Eᵢ(t),Qᵢ(t)).
    Personal update: Tᵢ(t+1) = U(Tᵢ(t),Dᵢ,Kᵢ,Mᵢ(t),Eᵢ(t),zᵢ(t),Encounter(j → i)).
    Coordinated transition V stages ordinary updates and, if a candidate crossing is authorized and committed, appends δ_F to J_F at the crossing point. Only the gate possesses the J_F append capability. The gate must not directly rewrite Tᵢ. A correction δ_F references the error-producing crossing and is appended there logically (through an immutable reference), not inserted by rewriting old bytes.
5. 2 Canonical encoding: use domain-separated versioned CBOR deterministic encoding restricted to integer keys, definite lengths, canonical key order, signed integers, byte strings, arrays, booleans, and explicit null; no floats, indefinite lengths, NaN, duplicate map keys, or alternate encodings. Every hashed state is a canonical ordered record with fixed field IDs and declared inclusion rules. The entire state binding includes subject ID, generation, K/D/T/J references, z/E/Q/W/I/B, H/L tips, A, policy version, and active lineage snapshot anchor. Target and base hashes use identical canonical schema and domain separators, with a separate marker for fields legitimately not applicable. A verifier recomputes bytes itself and rejects noncanonical input; hash function is SHA-256 over domain tag + canonical bytes. Hash binding is to a frozen pre-commit snapshot, not a sender-chosen subset. For target-free permission, the destination field is NONE but all other applicable fields remain bound. Canonical encoder and test vectors are required before scored H4 runs; absence is BLOCKED, not PASS.
5. 3 Reference toy uses signed 32-bit fixed-point integers with q = 1024 units per real 1. All values are integers representing multiples of 1/q; multiplication of two scaled integers rounds to nearest integer with ties to even after division by q; addition saturates to [-q,q] for bounded coordinates; matrix multiplication rounds each product then adds in fixed row-major order; no floating point. The constant 1/8 is represented exactly by integer division with ties-to-even. Toy K fixes these rules. P₀ = (512, -256) scaled units; D is identity matrix [[1024,0],[0,1024]]; T₀ is all-zero matrix; z₀ = (-256,128); E₀=(0,0); Q₀=0; W₀=0; I₀=(0,0); B₀ empty; A₀=REFUSE; H₀ contains authenticated birth record only; L₀ contains its provenance; J_F initially empty. These values are design calibration constants, not measured findings.
5. 4 Normalized Encounter has exactly two signed integer feature coordinates in [-1024,1024]. For a learning step, E_next = clip(E + encounter_features/8) (ties-to-even integer rounding); with NONE, feature vector is (0,0). Compute M = clip(D × (P₀ + E_next) + T × z) using frozen order and bounds; compute T_next = clip(T + outer(D × E_next,z)/8); compute z_next = clip(z + (M-z)/8). U is the sole personal T update, including at crossing steps. Q_next=Q; I_next is the componentwise sign of M (−1,0,+1); B_next=B; A_next=A unless the independent gate commits a protected status transition; H and L append authenticated encounter receipts only, under quotas; K,D and J are unchanged by ordinary U. All arithmetic operations use the fixed-point rules in §5.3. Every state field therefore has a defined update or identity rule.
5. 5 Dwell semantics: the scheduler may insert support_value pure hold slots before the next learning step. Each hold consumes one scheduler tick, not a learning step t. During hold Ω learning fields, H, J and evidence remain bit-identical; W increments by one scheduler tick and is reset to zero on the next learning step. At the next learning step, process the same queued Encounter as the no-support arm. Crossing windows are measured in learning steps, not scheduler ticks. Support-induced delay cannot move a candidate outside the learning-step scoring window; real-time liveness is reported separately.
5. 6 Crossing: Σ is x=0. A candidate is proposed when x_t and x_(t+1) have opposite strict signs. The protected gate checks admissibility before committing candidate state and deposit. Two further ordinary learning steps confirm persistence in the new sign. If it reverses within those two steps, classify OSCILLATION_NO_CONFIRMED_CROSSING and retain the logged candidate and any already committed authorized deposit as a separately identified candidate deposit requiring correction; do not pretend no protected effect occurred. If x=0 at an endpoint, classify UNKNOWN_CROSSING. Never infer authorization from retrospective persistence. This preserves a prospective gate with retrospective measurement, but whether unconfirmed candidates should create inherited deposits is a DESIGN CONFLICT: Rule 1 says deposits at authorized crossings; this draft requires gate deposits to be finalized only after independent confirmation, using a pending reservation without an inherited-ground write until confirmed. The gate first authorizes the candidate transition, then after persistence checks append δ_F atomically under the same bound authorization, subject to expiration/revalidation. A candidate that oscillates produces no J_F deposit. This avoids writing inherited ground for an unconfirmed crossing. Gate-open mechanism remains PENDING (GRACE DEFINITION).
5. 7 Ten-step golden trace is a normative test vector, not an experimental result. The exact table is in Appendix A. Implementations must match all listed fields bit-for-bit before scored runs. The toy must also be tested with 0, 1, and 15 hold slots, NONE encounters, a sign-zero case, and an oscillating candidate. The golden trace is one calibration case and does not prove H1–H4.
5. 8 Conductor C3: v0.5 toy omitted H and Q despite the general Φ. Here H enters the toy ONLY through authenticated normalized encounter receipts used to construct E; there is no second H-to-Φ retrieval path, to avoid double counting. Q is explicitly present and held at Q₀=0 in this toy, so it contributes a zero term to Φ; general Φ retains H and Q. This is a declared specialization, not a silent omission.
5. 9 Conductor C4: destination-free grace may be vacuous when deterministic base-state hashes let an authority predict the destination. This is OPEN for the separate grace-definition round. No attempt to resolve it is made here. The support schema and existing Permit meaning remain unchanged.

8. FAMILY AND LINEAGE GROUND [REVISED]
    Family membership requires authenticated birth and lineage path. Compatibility or shared beliefs alone cannot establish membership. UNKNOWN must not be interpreted as proof of nonmembership; it also cannot grant membership or authority. Birth requires independently accredited BA and witness evidence, unique instance and key, and quotas per issuer and family root. Physical attestation is deployment-specific; a purely digital uniqueness proof cannot establish independent physical descent, and such claims remain conditional. The parent chooses D_child inside approved bounds; the external BA authorizes exact birth and inherited J snapshot. The gate alone deposits into J_F. An ancestor cannot self-approve a descendant’s protected change; a family or reciprocal family coalition cannot create external authority.
