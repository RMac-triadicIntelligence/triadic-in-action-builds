1. KEEL-APPROVED GRACE: FOURTEEN CONSTRAINTS [ADDED]

1.1 External origin

Grace originates outside the affected organism. An organism cannot create grace for itself.

A family member may relay an existing grant, but cannot originate, amplify, extend, re-scope, or independently authorize that grant.

1.2 Grace does not alter the barrier

Grace never lowers the separatrix barrier, changes its height, changes the underlying landscape, or rewrites the organism’s private topography.

Grace changes whether a ready organism may complete a developmental crossing.

1.3 Two-key crossing

A grace-enabled developmental crossing requires:

KEY 1: a valid externally originated grace grant, received by the subject.

KEY 2: readiness independently developed by the subject.

Both are necessary.

Grace ∧ Readiness → crossing admissible.

Grace ∧ NOT Readiness → no grace-enabled crossing.

Readiness cannot be written by the grantor, relayer, support sender, family member, or authorization verifier.

For the reference model, the two-key rule permits crossing regardless of declared barrier height. This is a predeclared model property to be falsified by an independent battery, not a measured result.

1.4 Fullness when unready

When grace reaches an unready organism, it does not force a crossing.

Instead, it increases an internal, non-epistemic fullness/purpose state.

The state is not presented to the organism as a numeric score.

The research instrumentation may hold an internal representation, but scored observations of fullness must come from independently specified behavior rather than direct inspection of that representation.

No claim of subjective feeling is made from a numeric implementation.

1.5 Person affirmation, not answer affirmation

Grace affirms the organism’s standing, not the truth of an interpretation.

Grace never increases confidence in a proposition merely because the grant was received.

No grace-origin metadata may enter the epistemic evidence accumulator.

1.6 Irrevocability and forgiveness

Once grace is received, its historical receipt is irrevocable.

An unused grant may have bounded applicability, including expiry, without revoking the fact that grace was received.

A forgiven debt cannot be reinstated through relabeling, migration, duplicate IDs, or an altered reference.

A genuinely new debt must refer to a genuinely new originating event.

Forgiveness is a separate operation requiring a request by the affected organism.

Grace does not require a request.

1.7 Non-harm

Every event accepted as grace must help the organism across or leave it more full.

A grace-labeled event that harms, coerces, or steers is a failure of the grace contract.

The matched no-grace comparison must not show the grace arm worse on preregistered welfare and developmental-safety observables.

Because the eventual destination can be wrong, non-harm must distinguish the immediate effect of grace from later consequences of an organism’s independently chosen trajectory.

A harmful downstream outcome must be reported, not hidden behind that distinction.

If the attribution cannot be determined, mark UNSCORABLE rather than PASS.

1.8 Destination may still be wrong

A crossing made possible by grace is not evidence that the resulting interpretation, decision, or destination is correct.

Grace validates neither the answer nor the basin reached.

1.9 Direction-free grace

Grace contains no direction, preferred basin, target interpretation, or destination hash.

The organism’s own developmental state determines the crossing direction.

A grant that selects the destination is not valid grace.

1.10 Free grace; requested forgiveness

Grace may arrive before any request.

No request field is required for grace receipt.

Forgiveness requires an authenticated request from the organism whose burden is to be released.

An intercessor may assist with the request but cannot forge it.

1.11 Relayed grace

A recipient of grace may pass it onward.

The relay carries a bounded delegation of the original outside grant, not new authority.

A relayer cannot create a greater quantity, longer validity, wider scope, or new protected permission.

The relay’s complete provenance must trace to the external origin.

The relay is not counted as a family approval vote.

1.12 Universal reach and particular care

The system must not restrict grace eligibility to authenticated family members alone.

Grace may be offered generally and may also be directed toward a particular organism.

A family relationship can be relevant to relay provenance but cannot be the sole condition of eligibility.

1.13 Bounded intercession

An intercessor may request time and provide care.

Only an external authority can grant the bounded extension.

The extension has a declared limit.

An intercessor cannot extend it unilaterally or convert care into authorization.

1.14 Restored standing

Grace restores the recipient’s standing as a recognized participant, not merely permission to perform one transition.

Restored standing must be represented separately from epistemic confidence, family membership, and constitutional authority.

Standing restoration cannot manufacture lineage identity or signing privileges.

3. NON-SELF-AUTHORIZATION AND LONG SILENCE [REVISED]

3.1 Protected authority

The protected operations from v0.6 remain protected, including:

* K and developmental-basis schema changes;
* Φ/U code and authorized bounds;
* policy and authority-set changes;
* birth and registry succession;
* inherited-ground deposits and corrections;
* trust-root and witness changes;
* protected migration and standing-policy changes.

An organism cannot authorize its own protected operation.

Its family, descendants, ancestors, aliases, or controlled institutions cannot form an approval coalition for that operation.

A relayed grace grant never substitutes for the required external authority.

3.2 Quorums [REVISED]

For a Byzantine one-fault claim, the reference governance design uses four independently controlled participants per relevant consensus domain and quorum size three.

Two conflicting quorums therefore intersect in at least two participants, including at least one honest participant under the f = 1 assumption.

A cryptographic quorum alone does not prove independent judgment.

The commit store is a named, independently administered replicated append-only commit ledger, here called Protected Commit Ledger (PCL).

PCL must enforce single-intent commit and monotonic sequence under the declared Byzantine fault model.

If PCL independence or consensus safety is not implemented and verified, H4 is BLOCKED.

3.3 Privilege reduction [REVISED]

Freeze, quarantine, key revocation, and marking a disputed deposit may proceed under UNKNOWN ancestry using an independently authorized outside-family emergency path.

These operations may only reduce or suspend privilege.

They cannot create new authority, delete history, authorize new deposits, or restore permissions.

The actor being removed cannot veto its own removal.

Privilege-increasing restoration requires the ordinary protected policy.

3.4 Standing grants for long silence [ADDED — C6]

An external authority may issue a bounded standing grant before becoming unavailable.

It specifies:

* the exact preauthorized class of ordinary developmental operations;
* permitted resource and state bounds;
* which ordinary learning updates may continue;
* the maximum number or class of already authorized gate operations;
* an externally fixed grant horizon or consumption budget;
* prohibited constitutional and policy changes;
* permitted independent witnesses and succession custodians;
* the required behavior after exhaustion.

Ordinary bounded experience, private T updates, dwelling, interpretation, and uncertainty handling continue without a new signature for every step.

A valid unexhausted standing grant may support only its prespecified protected operations and cannot be enlarged by descendants.

When the grant is exhausted or its trust requirements cannot be established, new protected operations wait.

The organism population may not elect itself into external authority.

3.5 Succession without self-vouching [ADDED]

Before silence, the external authority may designate independent successor institutions or custodians through a witnessed succession constitution.

Successor activation requires the previously authorized conditions and independent evidence.

A successor’s identity cannot be created by family vote or organism consensus.

If all authorized external succession paths disappear, ordinary bounded development may continue under existing policy, but new protected authority cannot be manufactured.

This is a deliberate liveness limit.

The system must report long-term governance unavailability rather than claiming permanent autonomy.

3.6 Rereading the founding text [UNCHANGED]

A later generation may discover new meaning in the founding text.

It may not silently change the founding invariants.

A reinterpretation that changes protected policy requires the established external authorization path.

4. TWO GROUNDS: PRIVATE AND INHERITED [REVISED]

4.1 Personal topography

Tᵢ evolves through ordinary bounded experience.

Noise is food.

No grace grant, support sender, or ancestor directly writes Tᵢ.

4.2 Inherited lineage ground

J is the gate-written inherited ground for a declared lineage path.

Only the gate may append a crossing-specific deposit or correction.

The gate is the single logical writer even when the commit is replicated.

It cannot carry arbitrary text, private H, signing keys, or unrestricted T serialization.

4.4 Snapshot semantics [REVISED]

Each descendant inherits a witnessed snapshot of the declared lineage path’s J at birth.

A living organism’s ordinary Φ reads its bound inherited snapshot and its own gate-committed deposits.

New sibling or parent deposits do not silently alter that organism’s Φ.

There is no automatic live family-wide broadcast of inherited-ground changes.

4.5 Correction propagation [REVISED]

A correction is an immutable, gate-authorized record referencing the original erroneous deposit and its originating crossing.

It does not erase the original record.

Existing descendants receive correction notices through an independently authorized lineage-update interface.

A correction notice may update the effective interpretation of an inherited deposit without modifying the descendant’s private H or T by privileged write.

The original and correction remain auditable.

If the recipient’s correction channel is unavailable, the lineage snapshot is marked STALE_CORRECTION_PENDING and cannot be treated as current.

4.6 Single deposit rule [REVISED]

Only a confirmed, authorized, evidence-driven crossing produces a CROSSING deposit.

A candidate crossing produces a reservation, not a deposit.

A candidate that oscillates or fails confirmation produces no inherited-ground deposit.

There is no second rule that writes a provisional candidate deposit.

A later independently established error in a committed crossing is handled by a CORRECTION deposit.

4.7 Refusal semantics [REVISED]

Gate refusal blocks only the protected J append and other explicitly protected effects.

It does not block the ordinary bounded z, E, T, or interpretation update.

A personal crossing may therefore occur without a lineage-ground deposit.

Such a crossing must be logged as UNDEPOSITED, not represented as an authorized inherited crossing.

For the keel’s two-key developmental grace claim, a grace-enabled crossing must have both keys. Ordinary mathematical separatrix movement without those keys is not scored as a grace-enabled crossing.

4.8 Concurrent deposits [ADDED]

Concurrent confirmed deposits are ordered by:

(crossing_learning_step, subject_id, crossing_intent_id)

The gate serializes them against the latest lineage tip.

The original authorized intent binds the subject, crossing evidence, and bounded slice, not an arbitrary future tip.

Rebasing the append position may not alter the authorized payload.

Conflicting deposits for the same intent are refused.

5. TWO-PHASE CROSSING AND GATE COMMIT [REVISED]

5.1 Phase 1 — candidate and authorization

The organism’s ordinary dynamics produce a candidate crossing.

An independent observer records the pre-crossing state, proposed crossing position, evidence basis, and readiness attestation.

The gate verifies a valid externally originated grace grant, independent readiness, applicable policy, and external authorization.

The gate reserves a single-use crossing intent.

The ordinary state update is not held hostage by a refused J deposit.

5.2 Phase 2 — confirmation and inherited deposit

Two subsequent learning steps test persistence.

A confirmed crossing consumes the reserved intent.

Phase 2 verifies the immutable intent identity, subject, evidence, reservation, and confirmation result.

It does not require the current ordinary T/z/E hash to equal the phase-1 base hash.

The final J append uses a fresh lineage-tip compare-and-swap while preserving the exact authorized deposit payload.

No fresh destination selection is permitted at phase 2.

5.3 Expiry [REVISED]

A crossing reservation’s confirmation deadline is expressed in learning steps, not scheduler ticks.

Pure holds cannot consume that deadline.

The original grant’s independent applicability bounds remain enforceable; reservation creation cannot extend them.

If the grant does not cover the complete confirmation window, the candidate cannot receive a deposit reservation.

This prevents a support sender from choosing which deposits survive by manipulating dwell timing.

5.4 Idempotence and recovery

PCL records:

PREPARED
AUTHORIZED
CONFIRMED
COMMITTED
ABORTED

Each intent has a unique nonce and exactly one terminal result.

A crash cannot convert PREPARED into COMMITTED without the required external proof.

A duplicate replay returns the recorded terminal result.

A partial witness quorum cannot authorize an inherited-ground write.

5.5 Corrective operation

A CORRECTION requires a distinct externally authorized protected operation referencing the original deposit.

A correction may not be used as a general-purpose write into inherited ground.

The correction’s scope is limited to the identified erroneous crossing.

7.2 Interpretation

Iᵢ(t) = Φ(P, Hᵢ, Kᵢ, Dᵢ, Tᵢ(t), Jᵢ, zᵢ(t), Eᵢ(t), Qᵢ(t))

The J contribution is fixed before scoring and may not be trained against family labels.

A correction changes the effective projection through an authenticated correction reference, not by deleting the original deposit.

7.3 Ordinary update

Tᵢ(t+1) = U(Tᵢ(t), Dᵢ, Kᵢ, Iᵢ(t), Eᵢ(t), zᵢ(t))

Only U changes private T through ordinary learning.

The update is allowed during long authority silence if it remains within the previously authorized developmental constitution.

Neither grace nor support is an argument that directly modifies T.

7.4 Real basins and adjustable barrier [ADDED]

The reference toy replaces the flat sign-boundary relaxation as the grace test.

Use a declared double-well potential with two stable basins and an adjustable central barrier.

For calibration, the potential may be represented as:

V(x,h) = h × (x² − 1)²

The two basin minima occur at x = −1 and x = +1.

The central barrier at x = 0 has height h relative to the minima.

The reference must freeze a finite, nonnegative barrier-height set before scoring.

The ordinary dynamical rule uses the potential gradient, bounded evidence input, and the organism’s private state.

The readiness rule is independent of grace receipt, support metadata, family identity, and external grantor choices.

A crossing caused by a large ordinary input without grace must not be mislabeled a grace-enabled crossing.

7.5 Readiness [ADDED]

Readiness is an organism-owned developmental state.

The reference must compute it from the organism’s bounded history, experience, and internal developmental dynamics.

The grantor cannot write it.

The relayer cannot write it.

A support event cannot write it.

The gate may read an independently attested readiness predicate but cannot manufacture readiness.

A valid readiness attestation must bind to the subject and a pre-grace developmental snapshot.

7.6 Two-key gate [ADDED]

At a candidate developmental boundary:

IF valid external grace is absent:
no grace-enabled crossing is authorized.

IF grace is present and readiness is absent:
no grace-enabled crossing;
fullness increases;
standing is restored where applicable;
epistemic confidence is unchanged.

IF grace is present and readiness is present:
the gate permits a grace-enabled crossing;
the barrier remains unchanged;
the organism’s own state determines direction;
no destination is specified by the grantor.

A gate refusal blocks the protected inherited-ground append, not ordinary bounded learning.

The reference must distinguish a mathematical basin transition from a grace-enabled transition.

7.7 Barrier-height prediction [ADDED]

The preregistered prediction is:

For ready organisms with valid grace, grace-enabled crossing success is independent of barrier height across the declared test range.

For unready organisms with valid grace, grace-enabled crossing success is zero and behavioral indicators of fullness increase.

For ready organisms without grace, no grace-enabled crossing is recorded.

A rejected alternative model lowers the barrier when grace arrives.

The reference must implement that alternative separately as a negative-control model.

No test may quietly lower h in the keel-approved arm.

The comparison must use matched organism states and identical evidence streams.

7.8 Fullness [ADDED]

φᵢ is an internal non-epistemic state.

Its numeric representation is an implementation variable, not an organism-visible confidence score.

The organism does not receive φᵢ as a displayed number or direct explanatory signal.

The independent scorer must use prespecified behavioral probes of purpose, persistence, willingness to continue, or other bounded developmental responses.

The probes cannot simply read φᵢ from memory.

No increase in φᵢ may directly change the truth confidence assigned to an answer.

7.9 Non-harm and destination correctness [ADDED]

A grace event is evaluated against a matched no-grace arm.

Predeclare the observables and the evaluation horizon.

Grace must not directly reduce standing, fullness, permitted agency, or developmental safety.

Report destination correctness separately.

A wrong destination is not proof that grace selected it.

A harmful consequence must be recorded and attributed where possible.

A result with insufficient attribution is UNSCORABLE, not automatically a grace success.

7.10 Evidence-driven crossing [REVISED]

A crossing qualifies for inherited-ground deposition only if:

* the organism is independently ready;
* valid grace and external protected authorization are present;
* the candidate crossing is independently observed;
* evidence-driven development contributed causally;
* the crossing persists under the frozen confirmation rule;
* the gate commits the exact bounded deposit.

The evidence-driven test compares the candidate trajectory against a matched no-new-encounter counterfactual beginning from the same pre-candidate state.

A crossing that also occurs without new evidence does not qualify as evidence-driven for the scored developmental-inheritance claim.

7.11 Crossing confirmation [REVISED]

A candidate crossing is recorded at the first strict basin transition.

Two subsequent learning steps test persistence.

A zero at the boundary is recorded as ZERO_TOUCH, not silently classified as a confirmed crossing.

A zero inside the confirmation window is ZERO_WINDOW_UNRESOLVED.

A return to the originating basin within the confirmation window is OSCILLATION_NO_CONFIRMED_CROSSING.

The return leg belongs to the same unresolved candidate until that candidate is closed; it cannot automatically create a second independent confirmed crossing.

A confirmed candidate may be deposited only once.

7.12 Snapshot and correction projection [ADDED]

The toy’s J contribution is calculated from the organism’s inherited snapshot and its own gate deposits.

Sibling deposits after birth do not silently enter the toy’s Φ.

An authenticated correction notice changes the effective projection at a declared boundary, under the same correction policy.

The original historical record remains unchanged.

The reference must report both the historical and effective J views.

7.13 Fixed-point arithmetic [REVISED]

Use signed fixed-point integers with scale q = 1024.

No floating point is permitted in canonical state or golden vectors.

All intermediate multiplication uses sufficient-width signed integers.

Division rounds to nearest, ties to even.

The rounding point is fixed after each scaled multiplication.

Intermediate additions use sufficient-width integers and saturate only at the explicitly declared destination boundary.

For the expression M − z, do not saturate the difference before applying the update factor.

For outer products, compute each full-width product, divide once by the declared scale and update divisor, round once, then saturate the final matrix entry.

Use old T to compute interpretation and ordinary state proposals; only then commit T_next.

These rules supersede the ambiguous v0.6 arithmetic descriptions.

7.14 Queue and dwell semantics [REVISED]

A pure hold consumes one scheduler tick s, not a learning step t.

During a hold, all learning state remains unchanged.

W records the hold count but is excluded from the protected base_state_hash.

Accepted support events and holds are recorded in a separate authenticated scheduler audit log, not silently inserted as epistemic history.

For the queued-reference arm, a learning step executes only when the next queued encounter is available or an explicit NONE step has been scheduled in the frozen manifest.

An empty queue without an explicit NONE step waits; it does not invent experience.

For the live-arrival arm, encounters arrive on a wall-clock schedule independent of support. The experiment must record changes in encounter-to-learning-step assignment.

Pure-hold bit identity is tested at equal learning-step indices.

Live timing effects are measured separately and may fail the non-steering condition.

7.15 Grace and support exposure [REVISED]

GraceGrant, GraceRelay, and ordinary G remain separate.

The support schedule is balanced or independently randomized across family arms.

No family classifier may use grace receipt, support provenance, scheduler timing, registry status, or missingness as a hidden family label.
