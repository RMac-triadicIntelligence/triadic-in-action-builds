10. REVISED CONCLUSION [REVISED]

The historical analogy separates trust from verification; the governance design separates verification from authorization. Developmental continuity is not identical to copying autobiographical memory. The inherited basis may shape how individual topographies form; each organism has its own history and distinguishable trajectory. A family may offer bounded relational support, but no family member or family coalition may authorize protected changes to its own lineage. Support is a third event type, not a third authority type. A concrete, fair, independently scored battery must establish whether the proposed relational topography contributes predictive or efficiency value and whether family resemblance coexists with individual distinctiveness. These remain hypotheses, not results.
