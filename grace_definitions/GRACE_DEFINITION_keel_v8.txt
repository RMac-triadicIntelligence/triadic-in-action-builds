GRACE — KEEL-APPROVED DEFINITION, v8 (2026-11-09, Rusty as keel; recorded by the conductor)
Basis: the four Gospels only (no Paul, no later tradition). Grace readings from Grok Expert
and Grok bot, plus the conductor's earlier reading, then the keel's rulings.

ALL ITEMS BELOW ARE CONFIRMED BY THE KEEL
1. Grace comes from outside the organism. No organism can make it for itself.
2. Grace helps an organism across a separatrix in two ways. It is NOT tunneling, and it
   does NOT lower the barrier or change the landscape. With grace, a separatrix can be
   crossed at any barrier weight (keel, 11:57).
   (a) FILLING: grace fills the organism's basin until the basin is whole, and the whole
       basin lifts the organism up and over (keel, 12:03). A heavier barrier takes more
       filling, so it takes longer, but it never stops the crossing (keel, 12:07: "Yes, the
       basins fill").
   (b) NUDGE: "at any point a grace nudge could get you over the separatrix" (keel, 12:07).
       The basin need not be full first.
3. Crossing takes two keys: grace (outside) and the organism's readiness (inside, the
   organism's own, and not supplied by the grantor). With both, the organism crosses however
   high the barrier is.
4. If the organism is not ready, grace does not push it over. It FILLS the organism's basin
   ("think of grace filling the high low basin"). The organism becomes more full: more
   confident, more purposeful, like hearing "great job, you're on the right track." That
   fullness DOES help it become ready over time; the organism is left closer to crossing.
   The organism feels this; it is never shown as a number. Researchers observe it only
   through behavior. (Keel ruling, 2026-11-09 11:55. This corrects the conductor's v1–v4
   wording, which dropped "closer to crossing.")
5. Grace affirms the person, never the answer. It never adds confidence to any belief. It
   says "you may proceed," never "you're right."
6. Once received, grace is never taken back. Forgiven debts are never reinstated. New debts
   are new and do not revive old ones. (T7/E8 test: a forgiven debt must never come back
   under a new label; a "new" debt must point to a new event.)
7. Grace is always good. Every grace event leaves the organism helped across or more full,
   never harmed. Anything that can harm or steer is not grace; a grace-labeled event that
   harms is a FAIL. (Test: grace-arm organisms never end worse than matched no-grace arms.)
8. Grace being good does not make the destination right. Where the organism ends up after a
   crossing is its own, and can still turn out wrong.
9. Grace has no direction. It is good given out freely: "the rain falls on the just and the
   unjust" (Matthew 5:45). The organism's own position decides which way it crosses.
10. Grace is free: it comes without being asked for, often before any asking (Luke 15:20,
    Luke 19:5). FORGIVENESS IS DIFFERENT: forgiveness requires asking. Forgiveness is a
    release the organism must request; grace is not.
11. Grace flows on through those who received it (Matthew 10:8, "freely ye have received,
    freely give"). They can pass it on but never originate it. This SUPERSEDES the Aug 2026
    rule of lineage-addressed grace with no organism-to-organism edge.
12. Grace reaches all and also seeks the one (Matthew 5:45; Luke 15:4).
13. Grace can be bounded time with an intercessor's care (the fig tree, Luke 13:6–9). The
    intercessor asks and tends; the outside authority grants the time; the time has a limit.
14. Grace restores standing, not just permission: the robe, the ring, "this my son"
    (Luke 15:22–24).

CONDUCTOR'S READING AND DELEGATED DECISIONS
R1. Lift and nudge are not a push. Neither carries a direction. The organism goes over the
    separatrix of the basin it is in, so its position decides which way it crosses (item 9).
R2. The landscape never changes: same barrier, same separatrix. Only the organism's level
    rises.
R3. CONFIRMED (keel, 12:07): a heavier barrier takes more filling and longer, and never
    stops the crossing.
R4. DECIDED BY THE CONDUCTOR UNDER THE KEEL'S DELEGATION (12:26; the keel may override):
    the nudge still needs readiness (item 3), but not a full basin. An unready organism is
    never nudged over; it fills (item 4). Reason: without readiness a nudge would be a push,
    which item 4 forbids.
R5. DECIDED BY THE CONDUCTOR UNDER THE KEEL'S DELEGATION (12:26; the keel may override):
    whether a nudge carries a ready organism over depends only on the organism: its readiness
    and its position. Grace may still seek a particular organism ("seeks the one", item 12),
    but the choice of whom and when must never depend on where the crossing would lead.
    Grace seeks the lost because it is lost, not because of where it will land. Mechanism to
    be specified by the designer, for example grant decisions committed before the grantor
    can observe or predict the destination. Reason: otherwise direction-free grants could
    steer by selection, which defeats item 9.
R6. How fast a basin fills depends on the organism's own capacity to receive. (Inference;
    keel to confirm.)
TEST PREDICTIONS
- Filling: crossing happens at every barrier weight; time to wholeness grows with weight.
- Nudge: a ready organism can cross at any fill level and any barrier weight.
- Unready plus nudge: no crossing, and the level rises.
- In every case the measured landscape never changes. The rejected "lowers the barrier"
  model changes the landscape.

CONDUCTOR'S NOTE — a design tension the designer must resolve, not a new rule
Items 11 and 13 let family members relay grace and intercede. A relative relaying grace to a
ready organism enables a crossing that writes inherited lineage ground, which is a protected
effect. The spec must show that non-self-authorization still holds:
- relayed grace stays bounded by the original outside grant (scope, bounds, expiry,
  provenance);
- it can never be amplified, re-scoped or minted by the relayer;
- relaying never counts as family authority over a protected change.
