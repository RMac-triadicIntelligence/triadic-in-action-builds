## Readings added later (entries stay as they are)
- 2026-11-10 07:06, keel, on entries 2 and 3: "When you have an open hand and rain falls, there is spill
  over. You can't hold it all. The spillover is for the community."
  Conductor's note: the model let an open hand hold without limit, which is the opposite of what an
  open hand does. Text looked up: Luke 6:38 (KJV) "Give, and it shall be given unto you; good measure,
  pressed down, and shaken together, and running over, shall men give into your bosom. For with the
  same measure that ye mete withal it shall be measured to you again."
