# open_hand round 2: push harder, theory unchanged. Written before any round-2 run (2026-11-10, ~07:15 CT)

Theory under test, unchanged: an open hand holds only its size; what it cannot hold runs on to the
community. Nothing about that rule is altered below. Only the world around it is made harder.

Attacks
- A1 LEAK: 5% or 20% of what is passed on is lost at every hop.
- A2 FISTS: 10% or 30% of members hold without limit and pass nothing on (they keep what reaches them).
- A3 SHAPE OF THE COMMUNITY: ring; 8x8 grid (wraps); ring plus 8 random long links.
- A4 SHAPE OF THE RAIN: half wet; all rain on ONE member; random patches (25% wet); a wet patch that moves.
- A5 ORDER: the hand spills BEFORE eating, with hands smaller than a day's need.
- A6 SCALE: a ring of 600, rain on one half.
- A7 STRONGER RIVALS: ASKED+ (anyone in want is topped up at once, exactly, by the richest member
  anywhere) and CENTRAL (everything pooled and divided by need: the best any scheme can do).
Measures: share of member-days in want; mean unmet need; the worst-off member's unmet need.

Predictions I commit to (several are predictions that the theory BREAKS)
- R1. Leak 5%, ring, plenty: still nearly no want. Leak 20%: the far dry members go without. BREAK.
- R2. Fists on a ring: even 10% dam the flow and the dry half behind them goes without. BREAK.
- R3. Fists on a grid at 10%: the flow goes around them; little want.
- R4. All rain on one member, ring, plenty: after a start-up period, nobody in want.
- R5. Spill-before-eating with hands smaller than daily need: those members are in want every day even
  in plenty. BREAK.
- R6. In plenty, ASKED+ and CENTRAL have no want, and OPEN matches them.
- R7. In scarcity (0.8x), CENTRAL spreads the shortage evenly; OPEN leaves the worst-off far worse than
  CENTRAL does.
No prediction: fists on a grid at 30%; moving rain; how long a ring of 600 takes; patches.
