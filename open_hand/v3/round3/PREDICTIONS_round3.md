# open_hand round 3: a bigger community. Written before any round-3 run (2026-11-10, ~07:17 CT)
Rule unchanged: a hand holds only its size; the rest runs on to the four neighbours. Members eat first.
Community: a 300 x 300 grid, 90,000 members, each with its own need and hand size. One run of
1,000 x 1,000 = 1,000,000 members. "Want" is measured in windows of 500 days so settling can be seen.

Predictions I commit to
- B1. Plenty (1.3x), rain on half the grid: want keeps falling and is under 2% in the last window (day 12,000).
- B2. Plenty, rain in random patches (25% of members): want under 1% within 2,000 days.
- B3. 10% fists: want stays at or above 5%.
- B4. 5% lost per hop, rain on half: the far band stays in want; overall want at or above 20%.
- B5. 5% lost per hop, rain in patches: little want (under 5%), because nobody is far from rain.
- B6. Scarcity (0.8x), rain on half: a fed band next to the rain, and beyond it members miss nearly everything.
- B7. One million members, patches, plenty: same as B2 (under 1% within 2,000 days).
No prediction: all the rain on one member, at this size.
